Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Who we are

FishFinder (fishfinder.guide) is a free reference site that helps aquarium keepers choose fish, check compatibility, and care for their tanks. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

Information we collect

FishFinder does not require accounts, does not have login functionality, and does not ask you to submit personal information anywhere on the site. We do not collect names, email addresses, or payment details.

The only data collected is anonymous usage information gathered through Google Analytics, described below.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are popular and which countries visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and the pages you visit. IP addresses are not logged or stored by Google Analytics 4.

This data is aggregated and anonymous; we cannot identify you from it. We use it only to improve the site's content.

You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings. The site works fully without cookies.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load resources from the following third parties, which may see your IP address as part of serving those files:

  • Google Analytics (usage statistics)
  • Google Fonts (typography)
  • Tailwind CSS and unpkg CDNs (page styling and icons)

External links

Articles may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites and encourage you to read their policies.

Children's privacy

FishFinder is a general-audience reference site. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@fishfinder.guide.
