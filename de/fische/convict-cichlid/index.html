Über Convict Cichlid

The convict Buntbarsch is a tough, prolific breeder from the lakes und streams of Central America, ranging from Guatemala to Costa Rica. Its common name comes from the bold schwarz vertical bars on a gray Körper that suggest prison Streifens. Convicts are among the easiest Buntbarschs to breed, forming devoted pairs that aggressively defend their territory und offspring against all comers, regardless of size difference. Both parents participate in brood care, und fry can often be seen following their parents around the tank in a tight cloud. Convict Buntbarschs are robust, adaptable, und full of personality, but their aggressive Zucht behavior und territorial nature require careful tankmate selection.

Pflegetipps

Experten-Tipps

Convict Buntbarschs can be kept in a 30-gallon tank for a single pair, but größer is better when they start Zucht, as they become extremely aggressive toward all other fish. Provide caves und rocky territories mit a sand or fine gravel Substrat. They thrive across a wide range of conditions from 72-82°F und pH 6.5-8.0. Convicts are omnivorous und accept virtually any food, including pellets, flakes, frozen foods, und vegetables. Be prepared for them to breed frequently und prolifically. If you do not want constant Zucht, keep a single fish or same-sex group. Their aggression during Zucht should not be underestimated, even toward much größer tankmates.
