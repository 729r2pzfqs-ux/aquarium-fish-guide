Über Guppy

Guppies are klein, lebhaftly colored Lebendgebärende originally from the rivers und ponds of Trinidad, Barbados, und northeastern South America. Males display an extraordinary range of color Musters und Schwanz shapes, from solid reds und blues to multicolored mosaic Musters, making them one of the most genetically diverse ornamental fish. Females are größer und less farbenfroh but can store sperm for months, producing multiple broods from a single mating. Guppies were historically introduced to tropical waterways worldwide as mosquito control agents. They are exceptionally robust und adaptable, tolerating a wide range of water conditions, which makes them an ideal choice for beginner aquarists.

Pflegetipps

Experten-Tipps

Guppies breed prolifically, so keep an all-male group if you want to avoid population explosions, or provide dense floating plants like hornwort for fry to hide in. They prefer slightly hard, alkaline water und do well at a pH of 7.0-8.0. Feed a high-quality flake food supplemented mit occasional frozen or live foods for the best color. Males may harass females relentlessly, so maintain a ratio of at least two females per male if keeping mixed groups. Watch for common ailments like ich und Flossenbeißen, especially in overstocked tanks. Weekly 25% water changes keep them healthy und lebhaft.

Wie groß muss das Becken für Guppy sein?

Guppy braucht mindestens 38 Liter (10 Gallonen). Haltung im Schwarm ab 5 Tieren.

Welche Wasserwerte braucht Guppy?

Guppy fühlt sich bei 22-28 °C und einem pH-Wert von 7,0-8,0 wohl.

Ist Guppy für Anfänger geeignet?

Der Pflegeaufwand gilt als ideal für Einsteiger. Die Art wird etwa 2 Jahre alt und gilt als friedliche Art.
