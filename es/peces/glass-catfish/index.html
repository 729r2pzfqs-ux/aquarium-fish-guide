Sobre Pez Gato de Cristal

The glass catfish, also known as the phantom catfish, is a mesmerizing transparent fish from the rivers of Thailand y Southeast Asia. Its cuerpo is almost completely see-through, revealing its skeleton, organs, y swim bladder in impresionante detail. Glass catfish are unusual among catfish in that they are activo a media agua pez de cardumens rather than habitantes del fondo, hovering in place against a gentil current con subtle cuerpo undulations. They are entirely pacífico y somewhat delicado, preferring the company of their own kind in quiet, well-planted aquariums. When stressed or ill, their transparent bodies become cloudy or milky, providing an immediate visual indicator of their condition.

Consejos de Cuidado

Consejos de Expertos

Keep glass catfish en grupos of at least five, as solitary specimens become extremely stressed y often refuse to eat. They need a calm tank con gentil flow, plenty of plants, y no aggressive compañeros de tanque. Position them away from boisterous species like barbos y grande tetras. They prefer soft, slightly acidic water at 75-80°F y are sensitive to water quality fluctuations. Feed pequeño frozen foods like bloodworms, daphnia, y brine shrimp, as they may initially refuse dry foods. Glass catfish need time to acclimate y may hide for several days after being introduced to a new tank. Their transparency should be clear, not cloudy.
