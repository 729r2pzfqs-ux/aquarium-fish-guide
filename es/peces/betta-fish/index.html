Sobre Betta Fish

The betta, also known as the Siamese fighting fish, originates from the shallow rice paddies, floodplains, y slow-moving streams of Thailand, Cambodia, y Vietnam. Males are famous for their elaborate finnage y intense coloración, which have been selectively bred over centuries into dozens of cola types including halfmoon, crowntail, y plakat. Bettas possess a labyrinth organ that allows them to breathe atmospheric air, an adaptation to their oxygen-poor natural habitats. Males are highly territorial y will flare their gill covers y aletas as a threat display toward rivals. Despite their aggressive reputation toward other males, bettas can be surprisingly personable y often learn to recognize their keepers.

Consejos de Cuidado

Consejos de Expertos

Despite common misconceptions, bettas need at least a 5-gallon heated y filtered tank to thrive. Keep the water temperature stable between 76-82°F y maintain a gentil filter flow, as strong currents stress their long aletas. Never house two males together, y be cautious con female bettas as well unless you have experience con sorority setups. Provide floating plants or broad leaves near the surface where bettas like to rest. Feed a varied diet of quality betta pellets supplemented con frozen bloodworms or brine shrimp. Watch for fin rot, which often results from poor water quality or temperature drops.
