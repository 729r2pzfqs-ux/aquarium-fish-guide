Sobre Cíclido Convicto

The convict cíclido is a tough, prolific breeder from the lakes y streams of Central America, ranging from Guatemala to Costa Rica. Its common name comes from the bold negro vertical bars on a gray cuerpo that suggest prison rayas. Convicts are among the easiest cíclidos to breed, forming devoted pairs that aggressively defend their territory y offspring against all comers, regardless of size difference. Both parents participate in brood care, y fry can often be seen following their parents around the tank in a tight cloud. Convict cíclidos are resistente, adaptable, y full of personality, but their aggressive reproducción behavior y territorial nature require careful tankmate selection.

Consejos de Cuidado

Consejos de Expertos

Convict cíclidos can be kept in a 30-gallon tank for a single pair, but más grande is better when they start reproducción, as they become extremely aggressive toward all other fish. Provide caves y rocky territories con a sand or fine gravel sustrato. They thrive across a wide range of conditions from 72-82°F y pH 6.5-8.0. Convicts are omnivorous y accept virtually any food, including pellets, flakes, frozen foods, y vegetables. Be prepared for them to breed frequently y prolifically. If you do not want constant reproducción, keep a single fish or same-sex group. Their aggression during reproducción should not be underestimated, even toward much más grande compañeros de tanque.
