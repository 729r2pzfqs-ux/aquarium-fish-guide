Sobre Tetra Serpa

The serpae tetra is a fiery-red freshwater fish from the Amazon basin, found in the slow-moving backwaters y floodplain pools of Brazil, Argentina, y Paraguay. Its deep red-orange cuerpo is accented by a prominent negro comma-shaped mancha just behind the gill cover y dark edging on its aletas. Serpae tetras are activo, bold pez con a reputation for being more mordedor than many other tetra species, especially when kept in too-pequeño groups. In adequate numbers, they form a impresionante school of constant rojo motion. Their hardiness y vivid coloración have made them a staple in the hobby for decades, though their semi-aggressive nature requires thoughtful tankmate selection.

Consejos de Cuidado

Consejos de Expertos

Keep serpae tetras en grupos of at least eight or more to contain their mordedor behavior conin the school rather than directing it at otras especies. Avoid housing them con slow-moving, long-finned fish like bettas, angelfish, or fancy guppies. They prefer slightly soft, acidic water at 72-79°F y adapt well to a range of conditions. Provide a mix of open swimming space y planted areas. Feed a staple of quality flakes supplemented con frozen bloodworms y brine shrimp to maintain their deep rojo coloración. Their mordedor tendencies decrease significantly in más grande groups con ample space.

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Qué tamaño de acuario necesita el Tetra Serpa?

El Tetra Serpa necesita un mínimo de 57 litros (15 galones). Manténlos en bancos de 8 o más.

¿Qué parámetros de agua necesita el Tetra Serpa?

El Tetra Serpa prospera entre 22 y 26 °C con un pH de 5,5-7,5.

¿Es fácil de cuidar el Tetra Serpa?

Su cuidado se considera perfecto para principiantes. Vive unos 5 años y es una especie semiagresiva.
