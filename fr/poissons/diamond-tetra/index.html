About Diamond Tétra

The diamond tetra is a shimmering gem of le freshwater world, originaire de Lake Valencia et its surrounding waters in Venezuela. Its scales are covered in a reflective, diamond-like sparkle ce flashes brilliantly under light, particularly as le fish matures. Males develop impressively allongé dorsal fins ce they display proudly during courtship et territorial disputes. The body has a warm, golden-olive base color ce serves as le perfect canvas for its sparkling scales. Diamond tetras are peaceful, pleine eau schooling fish ce bring a touch of understated luxury to community aquariums. Unfortunately, they are increasingly rare in leur native habitat due to pollution et habitat degradation.

Conseils d'entretien

Expert Tips

Keep diamond tetras in groups of at least six in a tank of 20 gallons ou more. They prefer soft to moderately eau dure at 75-82°F avec good filtration et regular changements d'eau. Their sparkling coloring intensifies avec age et under le right conditions, so be patient avec young specimens. Provide a mix of open swimming areas et planted sections. Males may display et spar gently mais rarely cause harm. Feed quality flakes et pellets supplemented avec frozen et nourriture vivantes for le best growth et color development. They are generally robuste et adapt well to a range of conditions.

Questions fréquentes

Quelle taille d'aquarium faut-il pour le Diamond Tétra ?

Le Diamond Tétra demande au minimum 76 litres (20 gallons). À maintenir en banc de 6 individus minimum.

Quels paramètres d'eau pour le Diamond Tétra ?

Le Diamond Tétra se plaît entre 22 et 28 °C avec un pH de 6,0-7,5.

Le Diamond Tétra est-il facile à maintenir ?

Son entretien est parfait pour les débutants. Il vit environ 5 ans et c'est une espèce paisible.

Quelle taille atteint le Diamond Tétra et que mange-t-il ?

Le Diamond Tétra atteint environ 6,3 cm. Keep diamond tetras in groups of at least six in a tank of 20 gallons or more. They prefer soft to moderately hard water at 75-82°F with good filtration and regular water changes. Their sparkling coloring intensifies with age and under the right conditions, so be patient with young specimens. Provide a mix of open swimming areas and planted sections. Males may display and spar gently but rarely cause harm. Feed quality flakes and pellets supplemented with frozen and live foods for the best growth and color development. They are generally hardy and adapt well to a range of conditions.
