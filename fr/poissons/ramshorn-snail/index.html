About Ramshorn Escargot

Ramshorn snails are small, flat-coiled freshwater snails found worldwide, named for leur distinctive shell shape ce resembles a ram's curled horn. They come in a range of colors including red, pink, blue, et brown. Ramshorn snails are simultaneous hermaphrodites, meaning each individual possesses both male et female reproductive organs, et can reproduce rapidly under favorable conditions. They are primarily detritivores, feeding on algae, decaying plant matter, et leftover fish food. While some keepers consider them pests ce arrived uninvited on plants, others deliberately cultivate colonies for leur cleanup abilities et as supplemental food for pufferfish et assassin snails. Their flat, spiral shells et efficient scavenging behavior make them a useful, if sometimes overabundant, part of le aquarium équipe de nettoyage.

Expert Tips

Ramshorn snails are extremely robuste et will thrive in almost any aquarium conditions de 65-80°F. They reproduce rapidly when food is abundant, so control populations by reducing feeding et removing excess snails regularly. If overfeeding stops, populations naturally stabilize. They prefer neutral to eau alcaline avec adequate calcium for shell development. Feed primarily on algae et detritus, supplemented avec légumes blanchis et pastilles d'algues if needed. Ramshorn snails are harmless to healthy plants mais will consume dying ou dead leaves. They are sensible à copper-based medications. If you want to keep specific color varieties pure, house them separately, as mixed colonies produce random color offspring.

Questions fréquentes

Quelle taille d'aquarium faut-il pour le Ramshorn Escargot ?

Le Ramshorn Escargot demande au minimum 19 litres (5 gallons).

Quels paramètres d'eau pour le Ramshorn Escargot ?

Le Ramshorn Escargot se plaît entre 21 et 27 °C avec un pH de 7,0-8,0.

Le Ramshorn Escargot est-il facile à maintenir ?

Son entretien est parfait pour les débutants. Il vit environ 2 ans et c'est une espèce paisible.

Quelle taille atteint le Ramshorn Escargot et que mange-t-il ?

Le Ramshorn Escargot atteint environ 2,5 cm. Ramshorn snails are extremely hardy and will thrive in almost any aquarium conditions from 65-80°F. They reproduce rapidly when food is abundant, so control populations by reducing feeding and removing excess snails regularly. If overfeeding stops, populations naturally stabilize. They prefer neutral to alkaline water with adequate calcium for shell development. Feed primarily on algae and detritus, supplemented with blanched vegetables and algae wafers if needed. Ramshorn snails are harmless to healthy plants but will consume dying or dead leaves. They are sensitive to copper-based medications. If you want to keep specific color varieties pure, house them separately, as mixed colonies produce random color offspring.
