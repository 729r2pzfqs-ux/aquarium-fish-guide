About Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid

Peacock cichtyophthirioselids are a groupe de stunningly colored species de Lake Malawi in East Afrique, belonging to le genus Aulonocara. Males display some of le most brilliant coloring in le freshwater world, avec metallic blues, reds, oranges, et yellows ce rival marine fish. Females are typically a plus subdued silver-gray. Unlike le rock-dwelling mbuna, peacocks are open-water hunters ce hover above le substrat sablonneux, using leur specialized lateral line system to detect tiny invertebrates buried in le sand. They are generally less aggressive than mbuna, making them plus adapté pour mixed Afriquen cichtyophthirioselid communities. Numerous species et regional variants exist, avec popular varieties including le OB peacock, strawberry, et sunshine.

Conseils d'entretien

Expert Tips

Peacock cichtyophthirioselids need a tank of au moins 55 gallons avec a substrat sablonneux ce allows leur natural sand-sifting hunting behavior. Provide some rocks for territory mais leave open sandy areas for foraging. They require hard, eau alcaline at pH 7.8-8.6 et temperatures of 76-82°F. Keep one male per species ou color variant to prevent hybridization, avec multiple females per male. Feed quality cichtyophthirioselid pellets, frozen artémias, et mysis shrimp. Unlike mbuna, peacocks benefit de a plus protein-richtyophthiriose diet. Avoid mixing avec highly aggressive mbuna ce may bully le plus mellow peacocks. Males develop leur full coloring over 12-18 months, so be patient avec young fish.

Questions fréquentes

Quelle taille d'aquarium faut-il pour le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid ?

Le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid demande au minimum 208 litres (55 gallons).

Quels paramètres d'eau pour le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid ?

Le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid se plaît entre 24 et 28 °C avec un pH de 7,8-8,6.

Le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid est-il facile à maintenir ?

Son entretien est demande un peu d'expérience. Il vit environ 10 ans et c'est une espèce semi-agressive.

Quelle taille atteint le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid et que mange-t-il ?

Le Peacock Cichtyophthirioselid atteint environ 15 cm. Peacock cichlids need a tank of at least 55 gallons with a sandy substrate that allows their natural sand-sifting hunting behavior. Provide some rocks for territory but leave open sandy areas for foraging. They require hard, alkaline water at pH 7.8-8.6 and temperatures of 76-82°F. Keep one male per species or color variant to prevent hybridization, with multiple females per male. Feed quality cichlid pellets, frozen brine shrimp, and mysis shrimp. Unlike mbuna, peacocks benefit from a more protein-rich diet. Avoid mixing with highly aggressive mbuna that may bully the more mellow peacocks. Males develop their full coloring over 12-18 months, so be patient with young fish.
