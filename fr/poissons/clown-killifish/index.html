About Clown Killifish

The clown killifish is a tiny, brilliantly colored surface-dwelling fish de le shallow waters of Guinea, Liberia, et Sierra Leone in West Afrique. Its body displays bold, alternating bands of cream et dark brown, topped avec a spectacular fan-shaped tail fin painted in vivid blue, red, et yellow. Despite being a killifish, clown killis are not annual species et do not require a dry period for leur eggs, making them much easier to keep than many killifish. They inhabit le très surface layer of le water, where they hunt tiny insects et surface-dwelling invertebrates. At just 1.5 inches, they are parfait pour nano aquariums et add stunning color to le often-neglected top water level.

Conseils d'entretien

Expert Tips

Clown killifish are poisson de surfaces ce need floating plants et surface cover to feel secure. Keep them in a nano aquarium of au moins 5 gallons avec a couvercle bien ajusté, as they are skilled sauteurs. They prefer warm, soft, slightly eau acide at 73-79°F avec très gentle flow. Provide floating plants like water lettuce ou salvinia ce mimic leur natural habitat. Feed small floating foods including micro-pellets, crushed flakes, et live ou nourriture congelées like baby artémias et daphnies. Avoid strong filtration et surface agitation. Males may spar mildly mais rarely cause harm. They are peaceful avec autre tiny species et do well in planted nano community setups.

Questions fréquentes

Quelle taille d'aquarium faut-il pour le Clown Killifish ?

Le Clown Killifish demande au minimum 19 litres (5 gallons). À maintenir en banc de 6 individus minimum.

Quels paramètres d'eau pour le Clown Killifish ?

Le Clown Killifish se plaît entre 23 et 26 °C avec un pH de 6,0-7,5.

Le Clown Killifish est-il facile à maintenir ?

Son entretien est demande un peu d'expérience. Il vit environ 3 ans et c'est une espèce paisible.

Quelle taille atteint le Clown Killifish et que mange-t-il ?

Le Clown Killifish atteint environ 3,8 cm. Clown killifish are surface dwellers that need floating plants and surface cover to feel secure. Keep them in a nano tank of at least 5 gallons with a tight-fitting lid, as they are skilled jumpers. They prefer warm, soft, slightly acidic water at 73-79°F with very gentle flow. Provide floating plants like water lettuce or salvinia that mimic their natural habitat. Feed small floating foods including micro-pellets, crushed flakes, and live or frozen foods like baby brine shrimp and daphnia. Avoid strong filtration and surface agitation. Males may spar mildly but rarely cause harm. They are peaceful with other tiny species and do well in planted nano community setups.
