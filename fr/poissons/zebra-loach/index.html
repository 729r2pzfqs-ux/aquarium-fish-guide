Questions fréquentes

Quelle taille d'aquarium faut-il pour le Zebra Loche ?

Le Zebra Loche demande au minimum 114 litres (30 gallons). À maintenir en banc de 5 individus minimum.

Quels paramètres d'eau pour le Zebra Loche ?

Le Zebra Loche se plaît entre 23 et 26 °C avec un pH de 6,5-7,5.

Le Zebra Loche est-il facile à maintenir ?

Son entretien est demande un peu d'expérience. Il vit environ 15 ans et c'est une espèce paisible.

Quelle taille atteint le Zebra Loche et que mange-t-il ?

Le Zebra Loche atteint environ 10 cm. Keep zebra loaches in groups of at least four in a tank of 30 gallons or more with a sandy substrate and moderate current. They prefer clean, well-oxygenated water at 73-79°F. Provide plenty of caves and hiding spots using rocks and driftwood, as they are semi-nocturnal and appreciate daytime retreats. They are effective snail eaters and will control pest snail populations. Feed sinking pellets, wafers, and frozen bloodworms after the lights dim. Zebra loaches are generally peaceful with other species but may chase each other during hierarchy establishment. They are sensitive to poor water quality and need regular water changes. Cover filter intakes to prevent entrapment.
