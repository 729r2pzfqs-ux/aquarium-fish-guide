Frequently Asked Questions

What size tank does a Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid need?

A Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid needs a minimum tank size of 20 gallons (76 litres).

What water parameters does a Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid need?

Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid thrive in water between 73-84°F (23-29°C) with a pH of 6.0-7.5.

Are Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid easy to care for?

Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid are considered moderate to care for. They are semi-aggressive fish with a lifespan of about 5 years.

How big do Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid get and what do they eat?

Cockatoo Dwarf Cichlid reach about 3.5 inches and are omnivores. One male with multiple females, caves for breeding, soft water preferred.
