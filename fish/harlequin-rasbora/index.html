About Harlequin Rasbora

The harlequin rasbora is a graceful, copper-toned schooling fish native to the peat swamp forests and blackwater streams of Malaysia, Singapore, Sumatra, and Thailand. Its most distinctive feature is the dark, triangular or lamb-chop-shaped patch on the rear half of its body, set against a warm pinkish-copper background. Harlequin rasboras are true schooling fish that swim in tight, coordinated formations, creating a mesmerizing display of synchronized movement. They are among the most popular and widely available rasbora species in the aquarium trade. In the wild, they spawn by depositing eggs on the undersides of broad leaves, a behavior they will replicate in the aquarium with plants like cryptocorynes.

Care Tips

Expert Tips

Keep harlequin rasboras in groups of at least eight, as they are most confident and active in larger schools. They prefer soft, slightly acidic water at 73-82°F with subdued lighting and plenty of plants. A dark substrate and tannin-stained water bring out the richest coloring. These rasboras are peaceful and make ideal companions for other gentle community fish, dwarf shrimp, and small corydoras. Feed quality micro-pellets and flakes supplemented with frozen daphnia and brine shrimp. They are generally hardy once established but can be sensitive during initial acclimation, so drip-acclimate new fish slowly over at least an hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size tank does a Harlequin Rasbora need?

A Harlequin Rasbora needs a minimum tank size of 10 gallons (38 litres). They should be kept in schools of at least 8.

What water parameters does a Harlequin Rasbora need?

Harlequin Rasbora thrive in water between 72-81°F (22-27°C) with a pH of 6.0-7.5.

Are Harlequin Rasbora easy to care for?

Harlequin Rasbora are considered easy to care for. They are peaceful fish with a lifespan of about 6 years.
